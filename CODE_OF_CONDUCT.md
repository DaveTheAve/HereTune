# Code of conduct

HereTune welcomes contributors and users who can disagree about code without turning the issue tracker into a cage match.

Be respectful. Critique ideas and changes, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, dox, or deliberately humiliate others. Do not post private media, credentials, personal information, or other people's logs/data without permission.

Technical disagreement is welcome. "This algorithm is wrong because..." is useful. "You are an idiot" is not a benchmark.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict participation when behavior makes the community unsafe or unusable.

If you need to report conduct privately, contact the project maintainer rather than escalating publicly.
